# Compliance mapping

- **Product:** IDevelop Community Edition
- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Companion documents:** [`ASVS-L2.md`](ASVS-L2.md) (requirement-by-requirement verification), [`SECURITY-MEASURES.md`](SECURITY-MEASURES.md) (the control list, generated from `src/config/securityPosture.js`), [`SECURITY-AUDIT.md`](SECURITY-AUDIT.md) (internal code audit).

> **This is a self-assessment by the development team. It is not a certification, an attestation or an audit opinion.** It shows how the product's controls support the frameworks below, so that an organisation deploying IDevelop can reuse the evidence in its own compliance work. Certification (ISO/IEC 27001), attestation (SOC 2) and legal compliance (GDPR, EU AI Act) apply to **an organisation and its processes**, never to a piece of software on its own. The last section lists what the deploying organisation must still do.

How to read the tables:

- **Controls** are the product's controls, with the ids used on **About → Security** and in `SECURITY-MEASURES.md` (for example `identity/mfa`).
- **Evidence** names the code, or the Jest suite under `tests/unit/`, that proves the control. `tests/unit/securityPostureEvidence.test.js` fails when a cited file or suite disappears.
- **Support** is _Supported_ (the product provides the control), _Partial_ (the product provides part of it; the notes say what is missing), or _Deployer_ (the organisation must provide it; the product has no role or only helps).

## What the deploying organisation must still do

The product cannot do these for you. They are prerequisites for any certification, attestation or regulatory compliance claim.

1. **Policies and governance.** Information security policy, access-control policy (who is granted SuperAdmin, how access reviews are run), acceptable use, incident response and business continuity plans; an ISMS if you seek ISO/IEC 27001.
2. **Independent penetration test** of your installation (authenticated, every role) before production, and after major changes. See `SECURITY-AUDIT.md`, recommendation 1.
3. **DPIA** (GDPR Art. 35) for employee evaluation, 9-box and any use of the copilot; consultation of workers' representatives where national law requires it; your record of processing activities (Art. 30); an AI Act deployer assessment (Art. 26).
4. **Hosting controls.** TLS termination with a trusted certificate (then `COOKIE_SECURE=1`, `TRUST_PROXY`, and `SESSION_COOKIE_NAME=__Host-app.sid`), firewalling, OS hardening and patching, disk or database encryption at rest, NTP, and forwarding logs to a SIEM with alerting.
5. **Backups off-site.** The product's daily backups stay on the server; copy them off-site, encrypt them, and test restores on a schedule.
6. **Secrets custody.** Generate and store `SESSION_SECRET`, `APP_KEY` and the database password in a secrets manager; rotate them (`scripts/rotate-app-key.js`).
7. **Settings to review at go-live.** Idle timeout 30 minutes and absolute timeout 12 hours (ASVS 3.3.2), MFA required for every administrator (`mfaRequiredForPrivileged`), `COPILOT_BLOCK_PRIVATE_HOSTS=1` when the model is external, and `PASSWORD_REQUIRE_CHAR_CLASSES` only if your written policy mandates character classes.
8. **Vulnerability and change management.** Subscribe to releases, apply security updates promptly, and keep your own change records.
